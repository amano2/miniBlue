# Global Enterprise HR Policy: Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-002  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All Active Employees  

---

## 1. Objective and Overview
This document defines eligible business expenses, spending thresholds, submission deadlines, and approval workflows for operational and travel-related reimbursements.

---

## 2. Remote Work & Technology Allowances

### 2.1. Home Internet / Broadband Allowance
- Employees working under a hybrid or remote contract are entitled to claim up to **$50 USD per month** (or local currency equivalent) for high-speed home broadband expenses.
- Itemized billing statements showing the employee's name and billing address must be attached to each monthly claim.

### 2.2. Ergonomic Home Office Setup (One-time)
- Full-time employees are eligible for a **one-time $300 USD stipend** upon hire to purchase approved ergonomic furniture (office chair, desk riser, monitor arm).
- Claims must be filed within the first **90 days** of employment.

---

## 3. Business Travel Guidelines

### 3.1. Airfare and Rail Travel
- **Flights under 6 hours:** Economy class booking via corporate travel portal (Concur) is mandatory.
- **Flights over 6 hours (International):** Premium Economy is permitted with prior Department Head written approval.
- Domestic rail travel is reimbursable up to 2nd Class AC / Business Class for travel over 4 hours.

### 3.2. Daily Meal Allowances (Per Diem)
When traveling out-of-town on official business:
- **Tier 1 Metro Cities (e.g., New York, London, Tokyo, Mumbai):** Up to **$75 USD per day** for actual meal expenses.
- **Tier 2 / Non-Metro Cities:** Up to **$50 USD per day** for actual meal expenses.
- Alcohol is strictly non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized client entertainment dinner with Director-level pre-approval.

### 3.3. Lodging and Accommodation
- Corporate negotiated hotel rates must be booked through the travel desk.
- Hotel ceiling cap: **$180 USD per night** in Tier 1 cities and **$120 USD per night** in Tier 2 cities (excluding taxes).

---

## 4. Professional Development & Certifications
- Every employee has an annual Learning & Development budget of up to **$1,000 USD per calendar year**.
- Reimbursable items include relevant technical/domain certifications (AWS, GCP, PMP, etc.), industry conference passes, and approved technical courses.
- Pre-approval from the direct manager is required before purchasing or enrolling.

---

## 5. Expense Submission and Settlement Rules
1. **Submission Window:** All expense claims along with legible tax invoices/receipts must be filed within **30 calendar days** of incurring the expense. Claims older than 60 days will be rejected automatically.
2. **Receipt Threshold:** Original digital or scanned receipts are mandatory for all individual transactions exceeding **$15 USD**.
3. **Disbursement Cycle:** Approved claims are settled on the 15th and 30th of every month via direct salary deposit.
