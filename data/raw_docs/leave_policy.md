# Global Enterprise HR Policy: Leave and Time-Off Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees  

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the guidelines, entitlement, and approval procedures for all types of leave available to employees at the organization.

---

## 2. Types of Leave and Annual Entitlements

### 2.1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO / Earned Leave)
- **Entitlement:** All full-time employees accrue **20 days** of Annual Leave per calendar year (accrued at 1.67 days per full calendar month worked).
- **Carryover Limit:** A maximum of **5 unused Annual Leave days** can be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Any additional accrued days beyond 5 will lapse automatically on December 31.
- **Advance Notice Requirement:** Planned leaves exceeding 3 consecutive business days require at least **2 weeks prior notice** and manager approval via the HR Portal.

### 2.2. Sick and Medical Leave
- **Entitlement:** Employees are granted **10 days** of paid Sick Leave annually, credited on January 1 of each calendar year.
- **Medical Certification:** For any consecutive medical absence lasting **3 or more business days**, an official medical certificate from a licensed healthcare practitioner must be uploaded to the HR portal within 48 hours of returning to work.
- **Non-Accumulative:** Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and is not encashable upon resignation or termination.

### 2.3. Parental Leave
- **Maternity Leave:** Eligible female employees are entitled to **26 weeks (182 calendar days)** of fully paid maternity leave for up to two surviving children. A minimum of 80 days of service in the preceding 12 months is required.
- **Paternity / Secondary Caregiver Leave:** Eligible secondary caregivers receive **4 weeks (20 working days)** of fully paid paternity leave, which must be availed within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.
- **Adoption Leave:** Primary adoptive parents of a child below the age of 1 year are entitled to **12 weeks** of paid leave.

### 2.4. Bereavement Leave
- Employees may avail up to **5 consecutive business days** of paid bereavement leave in the event of the demise of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law).
- Up to **2 consecutive business days** of paid leave are granted for extended family members (grandparents, aunts, uncles).

### 2.5. Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
- Employees required to work on declared company holidays or weekends for critical project deliverables with prior manager pre-approval can claim a Comp-Off day.
- Comp-off must be utilized within **60 calendar days** from the date worked; otherwise, it expires.

---

## 3. Leave Application and Approval Workflow
1. All leave requests must be submitted through the internal **HRIS Portal**.
2. Requests for casual/planned leaves of 1–2 days must be submitted at least **48 hours in advance**.
3. In case of emergency or sudden illness, the employee must notify their direct reporting manager via email or Slack by **9:30 AM** on the morning of absence.
4. Managers must approve or reject leave requests within **3 business days** of submission.
