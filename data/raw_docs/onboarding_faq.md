# Employee Onboarding Guide & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Document ID:** HR-DOC-003  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Audience:** New Hires and Onboarding Coordinators  

---

## 1. First Week Essentials

### Q1: What should I expect on my first working day?
- **Orientation Schedule:** Day 1 begins at **9:30 AM** with a corporate welcome orientation led by the People Operations team.
- **IT Setup:** You will receive your corporate laptop credentials, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) setup prompt, and single sign-on (SSO) login info during the IT session at 11:00 AM.
- **Buddy Program:** You will be introduced to your designated "Onboarding Buddy" who will assist you with everyday workplace norms, tools, and introductions.

### Q2: What hardware and peripherals are provided to new joiners?
- Standard issue includes a choice between a 16-inch Apple MacBook Pro or a Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon.
- Accessories kit includes: 27-inch 4K external monitor, wireless mouse, ergonomic keyboard, noise-cancelling headset, and universal docking station.
- Replacement or upgrades for hardware can be requested through the IT Service Desk portal after 24 months of use.

### Q3: When and how do I enroll in health insurance and benefits?
- Benefits enrollment window is open for **30 calendar days** starting from your official Date of Joining.
- Coverage for health, dental, and vision insurance becomes active on the **1st day of the month immediately following your start date**.
- You can add eligible dependents (spouse, children under 26) through the Benefits Portal under the "Life Events & Enrollment" section.

---

## 2. Probation Period & Performance Milestones

### Q4: How long is the probationary period?
- The standard probation period for all new full-time employees is **90 calendar days (3 months)**.
- At the 45-day midpoint, a check-in meeting is conducted between the employee and manager to review progress against 30-60-90 day goals.
- A formal probation clearance review occurs before day 90. Upon successful completion, confirmation of employment is issued in writing.

### Q5: How is payroll processed, and when do I get paid?
- Salaries are disbursed on the **last working day of each calendar month**.
- Direct bank deposit details must be submitted in the HR Payroll portal by the **15th of your first month** to prevent payment processing delays.
- Monthly payslips are generated electronically and accessible via the Payroll section of the HR portal.

### Q6: What is the official working hours policy?
- Standard working hours are **9:00 AM to 6:00 PM** Monday through Friday, including a 1-hour lunch break.
- Core collaboration hours are **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM** across all regional teams, during which employees are expected to be reachable on Slack and available for team meetings.
