# Global Enterprise Code of Conduct and Workplace Ethics Policy

**Document ID:** HR-POL-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All Employees, Contractors, and Third-Party Vendors  

---

## 1. Principles of Professional Integrity and Mutual Respect

### 1.1. Equal Opportunity and Anti-Harassment (POSH)
- The organization is committed to providing a safe, inclusive, and harassment-free work environment regardless of race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or age.
- Zero Tolerance: Bullying, micro-aggressions, unwanted physical contact, sexual harassment, or discriminatory remarks will lead to immediate disciplinary inquiry and possible termination.
- Reporting: Concerns can be raised directly to the Ethics Committee or anonymously via the **SpeakUp Ethics Hotline** at `ethics-hotline@enterprise.org`.

### 1.2. Workplace Attire and Dress Code
- The organization follows a **Business Casual** dress code for regular office days.
- Formal business attire is required when meeting external clients, investors, or attending formal board meetings.

---

## 2. Conflicts of Interest and Outside Employment (Moonlighting)

### 2.1. Outside Employment / Secondary Commercial Activities
- Full-time employees are strictly prohibited from engaging in commercial secondary employment, consulting contracts, or board advisories that conflict with company business hours or domain competitors.
- Non-commercial, non-profit volunteer work or academic guest lectures are permissible provided written prior disclosure is approved by the Department Vice President and HR.

### 2.2. Gifts, Hospitality, and Anti-Bribery
- Employees may not solicit or accept personal gifts, hospitality, or entertainment from vendors, clients, or partners exceeding a value of **$50 USD**.
- Any gifts valued over $50 USD must be disclosed to the Compliance Team and either politely returned or placed in the communal company raffle.
- Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, prepaid debit cards) of any amount are strictly prohibited.

---

## 3. Information Security, Data Protection, and Social Media

### 3.1. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure
- Proprietary source code, customer personal data (PII), unannounced product roadmaps, and non-public financial metrics are confidential company assets.
- Confidential files must never be uploaded to unapproved public cloud drives or unauthorized third-party consumer AI tools.

### 3.2. Clean Desk and Screen Policy
- Employees must lock their workstation screens whenever stepping away from their desks (`Win + L` / `Cmd + Ctrl + Q`).
- Physical documents containing sensitive customer or employee information must be stored in locked pedestals or shredded after use.

### 3.3. Social Media Conduct
- Employees speaking on personal social media channels must include a clear disclaimer stating: *"Views expressed are solely my own and do not represent the views or opinions of my employer."*
- Posting confidential workplace photos, customer dashboards, or internal Slack messages on public channels is a severe violation of policy.
